1. Who we are

ANCHORFIX is an app developed by RDRP Studio (Rafael Del Rosal), based in Spain. For any question about this policy or your data, write to delrosalr@gmail.com.

2. In short

  • No user accounts, no sign-up.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • RDRP Studio has no servers that receive your data: your position, your anchoring and your settings stay on your phone.
  • The app only goes online to download maps and forecasts, and to send shore alerts if you turn them on.

3. Location

ANCHORFIX uses precise location (GPS) to show where you are on the map, find nearby anchorages and watch your anchor.

While the anchor watch is running, the app keeps reading your location in the background, also with the screen off, so it can warn you if the boat drags. During that time a permanent notification is shown, and background location stops when you end the watch.

Your position is processed on the device itself: it is not sent to RDRP Studio or stored on any server. The only exceptions are the ones you choose, described below (shore alerts and error reports).

4. Internet connections

The app only connects to these services:

  • Map zones. Maps are downloaded from GitHub, from a public RDRP Studio repository. The download does not include your position; as with any connection, GitHub receives your device’s IP address.
  • Weather forecast. Wind and pressure are downloaded from NOAA’s NOMADS service (USA), GFS model. To request the forecast, the app sends the bounds of an area of about 10° × 10° containing your position, not the exact position. NOAA receives that area and your IP address.
  • Alerts to a phone ashore (optional, off by default). If you turn it on, alarms and watch start/end notices are sent to the free ntfy.sh service on a channel with a randomly generated secret name. The message includes the alarm type, the distance to the anchor and the boat’s position with a Google Maps link. Anyone who knows the channel address can read the messages, so share it only with people you choose; you can replace it with a new one at any time. Messages pass through ntfy.sh servers, which keep them for a limited time under their own privacy policy.
  • External links. Mooring booking links, Google Maps and OpenStreetMap links open in your browser; what happens there is governed by each site’s policy.

5. Sharing the watch over wifi

The share feature sends the watch status (position, distance and alarms) to another phone or tablet on the same wifi network. It works without internet and only within that local network, and only the device that enters the pairing code receives data. So that the other device can find it, the app announces itself on the local network (mDNS).

6. Reporting an error

If you use “Report” to tell us about a map error or a mooring field, the app opens your email app with a prepared message that includes the position, the app version and the language. Nothing is sent until you send it. We use those emails only to reply to you and fix the maps, and we delete them if you ask.

7. Permissions

  • Location (precise, and in the background during the watch): explained in section 3.
  • Notifications: for the watch notification and the alarms.
  • Exact alarms, vibration, keeping the device awake and audio settings: so the drag alarm sounds on time and loud even when the phone is on silent.
  • Internet and network state: for the downloads and alerts in section 4.
  • Wifi and local network: to share the watch with other devices on board.

8. Data stored on your device

The app’s storage holds your settings, the downloaded map zones and forecasts, the anchor position and track of the current watch, the alert channel and the pairing code. If system backup is on, the system may include the app’s settings in your personal backup; RDRP Studio has no access to it. You can delete all data from the system settings or by uninstalling the app.

9. Purchases

If the app offers purchases or subscriptions, payment is handled by Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone). RDRP Studio does not receive your payment details: the app only receives the store’s confirmation that the purchase is active.

10. No ads, no third parties

ANCHORFIX contains no ads, analytics tools, trackers or third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

11. Your rights

Since we keep none of your data on servers, you are in control: you can withdraw permissions in the system settings, turn off shore alerts, clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If you have emailed us, you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of those messages at the contact address. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

12. Children

ANCHORFIX is not specifically aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from minors, in line with the fact that it does not collect personal data from any user.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new “last updated” date at the top.
