Everything you need to anchor

01

See the bottom before you anchor

Posidonia and other seagrass meadows, coral and reef shoals. Sand spots away from the meadows with their approximate depth, and a warning if the anchor or swing circle touches a protected meadow.

02

Mooring fields

Moorings in yellow if booking is required and blue if not, with who runs them, phone, VHF channel and booking link. Plus the areas where anchoring is forbidden. If something is missing or has changed, tell us right from the map.

03

Depth and chain

Contours and soundings, with a warning where the depth is only estimated. The anchoring calculator tells you how much chain to let out from the sounding, your boat and the expected wind, and where your swing circle will be.

04

Weather and wind shifts

Forecast wind on the map with animated particles and an hourly forecast for 72 h. Shelter of the anchorage for each wind direction, a barometer with trend, and alerts during the watch if dangerous wind is forecast or the pressure drops fast.

05

Anchor watch

Swing circle, boat track and distance to the anchor. A drag alarm that sounds even when the phone is on silent, with early detection before you leave the circle, plus GPS-loss and low-battery alarms.

06

Alerts to other phones

Alarms reach a phone ashore through the free ntfy app, no sign-up. Another phone or tablet on board can follow the watch over wifi, no internet needed.

07

Offline maps

Pick the zone of your next destination on the world map, download it before you leave and use it without internet.

148

Map zones worldwide. Mediterranean, Atlantic, Caribbean, Pacific, Indian Ocean and South America. Once downloaded, they work without internet.

In English, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Portuguese.

08

Built-in help

A complete offline guide: how to anchor well, how to read the seabed map, mooring fields, the alarms, weather and wind shifts, and how to stop the system from closing the watch.

Your anchorage is yours

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your position stays on your phone.
